final design

Ambient Interactive Visuals

Our concept turns a hospital waiting area into a calm, responsive nature space. A projector displays a natural scene, such as a forest clearing or a lake, across a wall, and the scene responds to patients as they move: leaves drift and water ripples where someone walks or reaches, and wildlife appears as people approach. Gentle nature-inspired sound, such as birdsong, flowing water or soft ambient tones, plays alongside the visuals. Body tracking (Kinect or MediaPipe) feeds the movement into TouchDesigner, which controls the visuals and audio in real time. The goal is to give patients waiting between treatments a quiet, optional way to explore and focus on something other than their anxiety, without needing a screen, a device or any instructions.
